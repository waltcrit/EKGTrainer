# Welcome to EKG Academy

EKG Academy is a structured, self-paced curriculum for learning to read electrocardiograms (EKGs, also called ECGs).

Whether you're a medical student encountering your first rhythm strip or a nurse practitioner brushing up on advanced arrhythmias, this curriculum is built around one principle:

> **Read every EKG the same way, every time.**

## What you'll learn

The curriculum is divided into three levels:

- **Beginner** — EKG fundamentals: how an ECG works, what each wave means, and a step-by-step method for reading any strip.
- **Intermediate** — Common arrhythmias: sinus rhythms, atrial rhythms, and ventricular ectopy.
- **Advanced** — Life-threatening rhythms, STEMI localization, and challenging patterns.

## How to use EKG Academy

1. Work through lessons in order — each one builds on the last.
2. After each lesson, open a strip in the **Trainer** and apply what you just learned.
3. Use the **Systematic EKG Read** checklist in the Trainer to build your read habit.
4. Mark lessons complete as you go to track your progress.

## A note on learning EKGs

EKG reading is a skill, not a fact-list. You build it by doing it — repeatedly, on real strips, with a systematic approach. The goal is not to memorize every pattern; it's to train your eye to ask the right questions in the right order.
