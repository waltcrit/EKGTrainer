# STEMI Localization

Once you identify ST elevation consistent with STEMI, the next step is to **localize the territory** — which coronary artery is occluded, and what myocardium is at risk. This determines the urgency, catheterization strategy, and expected complications.

## The principle

Each lead views a specific wall of the left ventricle. ST elevation in **≥ 2 contiguous leads** (measuring ≥ 1 mm in limb leads or ≥ 2 mm in precordial leads) indicates transmural ischemia in that territory.

Look for **reciprocal ST depression** in the opposite wall — this confirms the elevation is pathological (not pericarditis or early repolarization).

## Territory map

| Leads with STE | Wall | Artery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| II, III, aVF | **Inferior** | RCA (80%), LCx (20%) |
| V1–V4 | **Anterior/septal** | LAD (proximal) |
| V1–V2 | **Septal** | Septal branches of LAD |
| V3–V4 | **Anterior** | Mid-LAD |
| V1–V6, I, aVL | **Extensive anterior** | Proximal LAD |
| I, aVL, V5–V6 | **Lateral** | LCx or diagonal |
| V7–V9 (posterior) | **Posterior** | RCA or LCx |

## Inferior STEMI — always check right-sided leads

Inferior STEMI is due to RCA in ~80% of cases. If the RCA is large and dominant, it supplies the **RV** — right ventricular infarction (RVI) is present in ~50% of inferior STEMIs and changes management dramatically.

**Check V4R**: ST elevation ≥ 1 mm in V4R = RVI.

RVI management: fluids (RV is preload-dependent), avoid nitrates (cause severe hypotension), avoid diuretics.

## Posterior STEMI

True posterior infarction shows no STE in standard leads — instead:
- **ST depression in V1–V3** (reciprocal from the posterior wall)
- **Tall, broad R waves in V1–V2** (posterior Q wave equivalent)
- Confirmed by posterior leads **V7–V9**: STE ≥ 0.5 mm

Always apply posterior leads when V1–V3 shows horizontal ST depression.

## Anterior STEMI — LAD occlusion level

- **Proximal LAD** (before first diagonal): STE in V1–V6 + I, aVL; often with RBBB or new LBBB
- **Mid-LAD**: V3–V5
- **Distal LAD**: V4–V6; smaller territory
