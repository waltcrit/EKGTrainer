# Bundle Branch Blocks

When one of the main bundle branches (right or left) is blocked, the ventricle on that side must be depolarized cell-to-cell instead of through the fast conduction system — producing a **wide (> 120 ms), aberrant QRS**.

## Right Bundle Branch Block (RBBB)

The right ventricle depolarizes late. The delayed right ventricular forces produce terminal R' in the right precordial leads.

**Diagnostic criteria (all required):**
- QRS ≥ 120 ms
- **rSR' ("rabbit ears")** in V1 or V2 — classic RBBB pattern
- **Wide, slurred S wave** in I, aVL, V5, V6
- ST depression and T-wave inversion in V1–V3 (secondary, expected)

**Mnemonic:** RBBB = "**MaRRoW**" (M-shaped in V1, W-shaped in V6)

**Causes:** Normal variant, right heart strain (PE, pulmonary hypertension), anterior MI, RBBB ablation

## Left Bundle Branch Block (LBBB)

The left ventricle depolarizes late and abnormally. The septum depolarizes right-to-left (reverse of normal).

**Diagnostic criteria (all required):**
- QRS ≥ 120 ms
- **Broad, monophasic R** in I, aVL, V5, V6 — no septal Q waves
- **rS or QS pattern** in V1 (deep S or QS)
- **ST elevation** in V1–V3 and **ST depression** in I, aVL, V5–V6 (secondary changes — expected in LBBB)

**Mnemonic:** LBBB = "**WiLLiaM**" (W in V1, M in V6)

**Causes:** Coronary artery disease, LVH, cardiomyopathy, aortic stenosis, hypertension

## LBBB and STEMI

LBBB obscures ischemic ST changes — diagnosing STEMI in LBBB is challenging. Use the **Sgarbossa criteria**:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| ST elevation ≥ 1 mm concordant with QRS | +5 |
| ST depression ≥ 1 mm in V1–V3 | +3 |
| ST elevation ≥ 5 mm discordant with QRS | +2 |

Score ≥ 3 = high probability of STEMI. New LBBB in the right clinical context = activate the cath lab.

## Incomplete bundle branch blocks

QRS 110–120 ms with RBBB or LBBB morphology = **incomplete** BBB. Less severe conduction delay.
