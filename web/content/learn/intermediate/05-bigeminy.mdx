# Bigeminy & Trigeminy

**Bigeminy** and **trigeminy** are rhythm patterns where ectopic beats (PACs or PVCs) occur in a regular, repeating cycle with sinus beats. They are subtypes of "regularly irregular" rhythms.

## Bigeminy

Every other beat is an ectopic beat:
- **Ventricular bigeminy**: sinus–PVC–sinus–PVC…
- **Atrial bigeminy**: sinus–PAC–sinus–PAC…

On the strip: alternating narrow (sinus) and wide (PVC) or alternating normal and premature (PAC) beats in a perfectly repeating pattern.

The overall heart rate may look half what it is if you only count the sinus beats — always count all QRS complexes.

## Trigeminy

Every third beat is ectopic:
- Sinus–sinus–PVC–sinus–sinus–PVC…

The grouped appearance (groups of 3) is the key visual pattern.

## Why it matters

1. **Effective rate**: In ventricular bigeminy, every other beat is a PVC that may not produce a palpable pulse (no effective stroke volume). The patient may have a pulse of only 40 bpm despite an EKG rate of 80 bpm. Always check the **pulse rate** versus the **EKG rate**.

2. **Trigger for VT/VF**: Frequent bigeminy in the setting of ischemia increases risk.

3. **Cause identification**: Bigeminy is often triggered by:
   - Hypokalemia or hypomagnesemia
   - Digoxin toxicity (digitalis toxicity classically causes ventricular bigeminy)
   - Sympathomimetic drugs
   - Caffeine, alcohol

## Distinguishing bigeminy from 2:1 AV block

Both can show alternating narrow and blocked beats — but:
- **Bigeminy**: the PVC is early (premature), wide, and bizarre; compensatory pause follows
- **2:1 AV block**: normal P waves march through at constant rate; every other P has no QRS; QRS is narrow
